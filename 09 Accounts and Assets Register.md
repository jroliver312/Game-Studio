*Every account, domain, handle and asset the studio owns, who holds it, how it is protected and how it is recovered. This file holds **no passwords, keys or recovery codes** — only where they live. Checked at every monthly review (one random row) and in full every quarter.*

## 1. Rules

1. Every account is in the studio's name or the studio email where the service allows it, never a Thunderbuilt email or account (07 R-01).
2. Two-step sign-in on every account that offers it; a passkey or authenticator app before SMS.
3. Recovery codes go into the password manager **and** a sealed paper copy kept at home.
4. An account not in this register does not exist as far as the studio is concerned: add it the day it is made.
